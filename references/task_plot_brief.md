Title: Attention Network Test (ANT)
Construct: alerting / orienting / executive control

Purpose:
Show the participant-visible ANT trial sequence across cue types and flanker conflict.

Rows / conditions:
- No cue: fixation goes directly to the target flanker array.
- Center cue: center * cue appears before target.
- Double cue: upper and lower * cues appear before target.
- Spatial cue: upper or lower * cue predicts/marks a target location.

Timeline:
- Fixation: 500 ms; no scored response; centered +.
- Cue: 100 ms; no response; optional cue depending on condition.
- Flanker response: 1000 ms; respond to center arrow direction; F = left, J = right; congruent or incongruent 5-arrow array appears up or down.
- Feedback: 500 ms; no response; correct, incorrect, or no response.
- ITI: random 800-1200 ms; no response; blank screen.

Notes:
- No-cue trials omit the cue phase.
- Participant responds only to the center arrow and ignores flankers.
- Congruent examples use <<<<< or >>>>>; incongruent examples use >><>> or <<><<.
